Chapter Four · The Silent Harbour The Shadow of the Wind · Carlos Ruiz Zafón

I never learned if the damp morning wind had swept across the cathedrals from the open sea, or if it had been exhaled by the stones themselves. In those hours, shadows clung to the iron balconies like damp cloth, and the salt mist hung low over the littoral stones where forgotten vessels lay dormant in the twilight. Everything seemed constructed of quiet dust and dried sea salt.

The sound of the bell tower reached the docks muffled, as though the heavy air were swallowing every vibration before it could pierce the cemetery gates.

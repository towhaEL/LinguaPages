The Shadow of the Wind · Carlos Ruiz Zafón

Chapter Four: The Silent Harbour

❦

I still remember the day that my father took me to the Cemetery of Forgotten Books for the first time. The early summer fog lingered in the cobblestones of Rambla de Santa Mónica like wet spun glass, veiling the gas lanterns in spectral halos.

We stepped into the vaulted darkness, and my senses were enveloped by the intoxicating scent of ancient glue, damp parchment, and resting memories. Countless shelves ascended toward the skylights like cathedrals of untended wisdom, housing millions of discarded souls that waited for a living hand to bring their echoes back into the waking world.

‘Daniel,’ my father whispered as he knelt beside me, his palm resting upon my shoulder. ‘Every book you see here has been someone’s dearest secret, someone’s sleepless night, someone’s sanctuary. When a library closes or an attic is emptied, they find their way to this sanctuary. And today, you may choose one to adopt.’

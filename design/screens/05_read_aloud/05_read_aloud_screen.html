The Silent Harbour

The city of Barcelona still held its secrets in the damp morning mist, creeping up through the cobblestones of Calle Arco del Teatro.

We walked through the sleeping alleys without exchanging a single word. I still remember the day that my father took me to the Cemetery of Forgotten Books for the first time. It was the early summer of 1945, and we walked through the streets of a city veiled in charcoal skies.

"Daniel, what you are about to see today is a mystery very few souls ever encounter," my father whispered as his grip on my hand tightened near the archway of the old palace.

What we heard

The brief

An offline-first, private ebook reader for every DRM-free format (EPUB, PDF, TXT, Markdown, HTML, FB2), for heavy readers and for researchers who annotate. It should feel calm, trustworthy and premium-dark, and stay clear of store-style reading apps and the open-book-on-orange cliché.

calmtrustworthyprivatesimple

Success: ownable among reading apps, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour, sits naturally in the app's dark Paperback Bands UI.

Final design

Bookmark p

The p of Pages, its tail cut like a bookmark ribbon: your place, kept.

  • Says reading without the open-book cliché, and stays clear of orange open-book app icons
  • The ribbon echoes what the app protects: bookmarks, highlights and notes that are never lost
  • Built from one ring, one stem and one 45° cut; a heavier small-size cut keeps the notch at 16 px
  • Paper and press-orange on ink, taken straight from the app's design tokens

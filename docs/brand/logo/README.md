# OpenPages — Logo Guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** the lowercase **p** of *Pages*, its tail cut like a bookmark ribbon: your place, kept.
- **Parts:** one ring (the bowl), one stem, and the **ribbon**: the part of the stem below the bowl, ending in a 45° notch. Only the ribbon takes press-orange.
- **Versions:** horizontal (primary) · stacked · symbol · wordmark. Each comes in `color-dark` (for ink backgrounds), `color-light` (for light backgrounds), `black` and `white`.
- **Small-size cut:** `openpages-symbol-small-*` has a heavier stem, a smaller counter and a deeper notch. Use it whenever the symbol is drawn under 48 px. The app icon, favicon and web icons already use it.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `masters/` | SVG masters: every lockup × colour scheme, the small cut, the app icon (rounded and full-bleed), and the Android adaptive foreground, background and monochrome layers (432 × 432, mark inside the 66/108 safe zone) |
| `png/` | Transparent 1024 px symbol and app icon PNGs; lockups at 2× their native size |
| `web/` | `favicon.ico`, `favicon.svg`, 16/32/48 PNGs, apple-touch, 192/512, maskable, `site.webmanifest`, `head-snippet.html` |
| `presentation.html`, `slides/` | Final-design presentation board with mockups (one PNG per slide) |
| `presentation-spec.json` | Source for regenerating the board |

## 2. Clear space
Keep a clear zone of **1 × stem width** (the width of the p's stem) around the logo on all sides. The zone scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 120 px wide | 30 mm wide |
| Stacked | 64 px wide | 18 mm wide |
| Symbol | 16 px (small cut below 48 px) | 5 mm tall |

## 4. Colour
Taken from the app's design tokens (`DESIGN.md`).

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#111315` | 17 19 21 | 19 10 0 92 | Logo on light backgrounds; app-icon tile |
| Paper | `#ECEAE4` | 236 234 228 | 0 1 3 7 | Logo on ink / dark backgrounds |
| Press orange | `#E0742E` | 224 116 46 | 0 48 79 12 | Ribbon on dark (5.9:1 on ink) |
| Press orange, light | `#C9601C` | 201 96 28 | 0 52 86 21 | Ribbon on light (4.1:1 on white) |

CMYK values are naive conversions: ask the printer for a proof. No Pantone match has been chosen yet.

**Approved pairs:** paper + orange on ink (primary, matches the app) · ink + light orange on white or paper · all-white on ink or a photo · all-black on white.
**One-colour rule:** if only one colour is available, the whole p, ribbon included, takes that colour. Never draw the ribbon as a gap.

## 5. Typography
- **Wordmark:** Figtree Bold, tracking −10/1000, converted to outlines. Never retype it.
- **UI and headlines:** Figtree (the app's typeface). Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/figtree/OFL.txt`), which permits logo use.

## 6. Don'ts
Don't stretch, rotate or outline the mark · don't colour the bowl orange or the ribbon anything but orange or the one-colour fill · don't add shadows, gradients or textures (the system is flat) · don't rearrange or resize the parts of a lockup · don't use the master symbol below 48 px (use the small cut) · don't place the paper version on light backgrounds.

## 7. Notes and open items
- **Trademark:** not cleared. Run a professional search (EUIPO/USPTO, plus a reverse image search). Lowercase-p marks are common.
- **Name:** the code still says "LinguaPage". The app label and launcher icons haven't been changed yet.
- **Wordmark:** stock Figtree. A custom detail (for example a ribbon-notched P in *Pages*) could make it more ownable later.
- **Mockups:** the names, handles and domain on the presentation board are placeholders.
- **Rebuilding:** the masters are generated by `build_kit.py` (fontTools + Figtree). The geometry is: stem 30 × 192 units, bowl r 66, counter 31 × 34, ribbon split at y 164, 45° notch. All of it is on a 256 grid.
